## 7. ASQA metric — investigated, not acted on

Why the proxy exists: `DR = sqrt(ROUGE-L × Disambig-F1)`, and Disambig-F1 needs a
RoBERTa-SQuADv2 reader. `DR* = sqrt(ROUGE-L × STR-EM)` substitutes STR-EM.

The ASQA authors' statement that STR-EM and Disambig-F1 measure the same aspect
justifies *excluding* STR-EM from DR, not *swapping it in*. A reviewer who works
on ASQA will say so.

**Nothing blocks switching.** Disambiguated questions are in `asqa_dev.parquet`
(`qa_pairs[i]['question']`), torch 2.13 and transformers 5.15 are installed, MPS
is available. Only `deepset/roberta-base-squad2` needs downloading (~500 MB).

**Cost is the threshold sweep, not the reader.** `asqa_ours.log` records 251,304
distinct DR evaluations for one arm's sweep; at 3.36 interpretations each that is
~844k reader passes, per arm.

**The option that works: tune on DR\*, report on DR.** Compute the official metric
once, on final selections only.

| scope | reader passes | runtime on MPS |
|---|---|---|
| headline 6-agent table, ~5 arms | ~6,700 | under a minute |
| all 42 ensembles × 5 arms | ~282,000 | tens of minutes |
| full re-tune under DR | ~10⁶ per sweep | not worth it |

This also lets you correlate DR* against DR and say with a number whether the
substitution changed any conclusion.
