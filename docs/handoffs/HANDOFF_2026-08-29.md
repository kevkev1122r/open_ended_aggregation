### The page model is unreliable for captions

`pages ≈ rendered_chars / 4100 + 0.34 × figures + 0.20 × tables` prices every figure at a
flat 0.34 **regardless of caption length**. A 7-line caption and a 2-line caption score
identically. When the author reported "six lines too far" the model showed 0.01 pp of
slack; the actual fix was cutting a 618-character caption to 330. **Trust the Overleaf
page count, not this formula, for anything under half a page.**
