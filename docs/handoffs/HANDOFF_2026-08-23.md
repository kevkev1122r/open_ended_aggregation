## 2. What is settled and should not be relitigated

- **The ceiling argument.** Every reliability-weighting rule is a function of the
  support pattern, so `P(correct | exact subset asserted)` upper-bounds all of
  them — including correlation- and omission-aware ones, since the pattern also
  determines who stayed silent. This is a construction, not an empirical claim.
- **Verbosity is not a claim.** It adds +0.01 over position and is ablated out.
  List length survives only inside omission strength, where a silent agent has no
  position and length is the only available evidence.
- **k is not known and does not need to be.** Ĝ's mean absolute error is 4.62
  against a gold mean of 10.2; the true count gains +0.55; a constant k gains
  nothing. The cut must *vary*, not be accurate.
- **Everything is supervised.** So is every baseline (count's θ, ASC's Θ, OW's
  weights). Fair comparison, but our label appetite is larger — ~115 coefficients
  versus one integer. Belongs in Limitations.
- **Novelty check done** — 25+ sources, `docs/NOVELTY_CHECK.md`. Dawid–Skene must
  be cited and our `pattern`/`nb-full` arms presented as *its* baselines. 15 of 23
  references still need direct reads.

## 3. THE OPEN PROBLEM — the method reads as QAMPARI-specific

**Supervisor, 23 Aug:** *"This currently reads as a very specific method for
QAMPARI or at best QAMPARI-like datasets. Ideally we want an argument about a
method where potentially some specific feature is dataset-optimised, but the
general pattern would work across other long-answer tasks."*

He is right, and the ASQA sweep makes it sharper rather than softer. Do not try
to argue him out of it. The task is to find the framing the evidence actually
supports.

### 3.1 What is genuinely general, with evidence

| claim | QAMPARI | ASQA | TriviaQA |
|---|---|---|---|
| the pattern lookup bounds all weighting | yes, by construction | yes | yes |
| **whether that bound is binding** | binding (+0.25 ns) | slack (+2.44 *) | slack (+0.94 *) |
| solo ratio predicts which | 0.06 | 0.47 | 0.24 |
| some output-structure signal exists | position, omission | sentence position | self-consistency |
| that signal beats weighting | **yes** | **no** | **yes** (+0.76 * vs OW) |

Two things transfer cleanly:
- **The ceiling construction.** Format-independent. What varies is whether it
  binds, and the solo ratio predicts that in advance from cached data.
- **The existence of an output-structure signal.** Every benchmark has one; on
  two of three it beats the weighting it competes with.

One thing does not transfer: **the specific features.** Position + omission +
budget is a QAMPARI instantiation. Sentence position on prose does not clear the
ceiling; position and budget are undefined for single answers.

### 3.2 Three candidate reframings

**A — Pluggable confidence signals (recommended).** The method is: *estimate
per-claim P(correct) from whatever per-agent confidence signal the answer format
affords, then apply a decision rule.* The signal slot is explicitly
task-dependent — that is the "dataset-optimised feature" he is asking for — while
the surrounding machinery (claim pooling, cross-fitting, the ceiling comparison,
the decision rule) is fixed. Three instantiations, one method.
*Needs:* a common template implemented identically across all three benchmarks so
the paper shows one pipeline with a swapped slot, not three separate studies.

**B — A design-space paper.** Three classes of rule (count / support pattern /
output structure), a proof that class B is capped, and the solo ratio as the
selection rule. Contributions are the taxonomy and the selector; the QAMPARI
method is the worked example.
*Needs:* the solo ratio validated on a fourth dataset — three points is an
observation, not a law.

**C — Honest scope narrowing.** Claim "enumerative long-form QA" and present
ASQA/TriviaQA as boundary studies showing where the mechanism stops.
*Needs:* nothing new. Weakest but safest, and still publishable.

**My read:** A is the right target and B is the fallback. A is only credible if
the three benchmarks run through visibly the same code path — right now they run
through `weighting_v5.py`, `asqa_ours_ensembles.py` and `triviaqa_selfcons.py`,
which *look* like three different methods even though they share a structure.
That appearance is most of the problem.

### 3.3 Concrete next experiments, in priority order

1. **Unify the pipeline.** One module, one feature-assembly interface, three
   signal plugins. Purely a refactor of existing code — no new results — but it
   is what makes framing A truthful rather than rhetorical.
2. **Fourth dataset for the solo-ratio screen.** Cheapest real validation of the
   general claim. Run the screen *before* implementing anything for it.
3. **Multi-sample generation on QAMPARI or ASQA** so self-consistency (the signal
   that works on TriviaQA) becomes available on a set-valued task. This is the
   single experiment that would most directly test "the general pattern works
   across long-answer tasks," because it ports a signal *across* formats. Needs
   generation: 777 × m calls, inside the $30 cap at m=20 with a cheap model.
4. **Report the ASQA loss prominently, not defensively.** Two mirror-image
   sweeps with opposite verdicts, predicted in advance by one number, is a
   stronger result than one sweep we win.

### Traps that have already cost us

- **Threshold tuning on the evaluation set flips signs** — +0.08 became −1.25.
  Nothing tuned in-sample is a result.
- **Tie handling in threshold sweeps.** Picking the argmax over every position
  lets a sweep score a partial tie group it can never keep; it robs whichever arm
  has the most ties, which is `count`. Fixed in `rank_crossover.py`; the fix is
  what made it reproduce `ensemble_sizes.py`.
- **TriviaQA `natural` responses have no answer marker.** String-matching them
  compares 23-word sentences and produces a bogus 91.7% solo rate. Use `terse`.
- **`methods/asc.py` was O(n²) over unique items** and one question has 3,305 of
  them. Length-window blocking makes it exact and 14 minutes instead of hours.
- **QAMPARI gold ships no aliases for 97.2% of entries** — depresses every arm
  roughly uniformly. Disclose; do not silently relax.

## 6. The claim to defend, as currently supported

> Reliability weighting over agents — including its classical Dawid–Skene
> ceiling — is provably bounded on set-valued aggregation, and the bound is
> computable in advance from the solo ratio. Where the bound binds (QAMPARI), the
> remaining signal lies in the structure of each agent's own output, and
> exploiting it beats counting in 210 of 210 ensembles. Where the bound is slack
> (ASQA), weighting is the better method and we show that too.

**The open question is whether "the structure of each agent's own output" can be
stated as one method with a task-dependent slot, or only as three related
methods.** That is §3, and it is what to work on next.
